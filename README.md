# 🛡️ SAKSHAM

### Learn. Practice. Measure. Respond.

> **A digital disaster preparedness and response platform designed for schools and colleges.**

SAKSHAM transforms disaster preparedness from passive awareness into **active, measurable readiness**.

Instead of simply telling students what to do during an emergency, SAKSHAM enables them to **learn safety procedures, practice through realistic scenarios, assess their performance, and continuously improve their preparedness.**

---

## 🚨 Why SAKSHAM?

Disaster preparedness in educational institutions often depends on:

- 📚 Theoretical learning
- 📋 Occasional drills
- 📄 Scattered emergency information
- ❌ Limited performance measurement

> **Knowing what to do is different from being prepared to do it.**

SAKSHAM bridges this gap by bringing **learning, simulation, assessment, emergency resources, and institutional preparedness** into one platform.

---

# ✨ Core Features

### 🎓 Student Dashboard

Students can:

- View their preparedness progress
- Continue disaster-learning modules
- Take quizzes and assessments
- Practice emergency scenarios
- Track their SAKSHAM Readiness Score
- Identify areas that need improvement
- Access emergency resources

---

### 📚 Interactive Disaster Learning

Students can learn about different emergency situations through structured modules covering:

- 🌍 Earthquakes
- 🌊 Floods
- 🔥 Fire Emergencies
